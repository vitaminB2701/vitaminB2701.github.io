Publication on ChemPhysChem

---

We recently published an article on ChemPhysChem, focusing on technical aspects of polarization-resolved two-dimensional electronic spectroscopy (p-2DES). We demonstrated an implementation of general p-2DES for simultaneous acquisition of polarized 2D spectra. These spectra can be linearly combined in post-processing to obtain spectra of other polarization schemes: isotropic dynamics, anisotropy dynamics, cross-peak specific, diagonal peak enhance, etc. You name it. All in one experiment! 

Take a look <a href="https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/cphc.202500903">here</a> for the article. The full text is available in my publication list.
